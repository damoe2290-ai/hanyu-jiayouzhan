# Lesson 7 pictures: decision record

Lesson 7 («中国银行在哪儿», `lesson-7.html`) uses the site's own hand-drawn SVG icons (`ICON` in `tools/lsn3/legacy/g7.data.js`).

## History
1. **Phase 4a (PR #11):** 11 Wikimedia Commons photos for the place words, plus new drawings for the position words. The owner did not like the photos.
2. **Phase 4b (PR #12, closed without merging):** two illustrated versions for 图书馆 银行 邮局: scenes drawn for the site (A) and Openclipart clip art (B). The owner liked neither.
3. **Decision:** back to the original icons for all place words, exactly as before Phase 4a. No photos, illustrations or clip art remain in the repository.

## Kept from Phase 4a
- Drawings for the 11 position and distance words 左边 右边 前边 后边 上边 下边 里边 外边 旁边 这儿 那儿 (and their entries in the picture quiz pool, which grew from 18 to 29 words).
- Redrawn 知道 (a head with a check mark) and 没关系 (thumbs up).
- The 360px layout fix for the vocabulary rows (运动场, 中国银行 were wider than the screen and pushed the speaker button off it).

## Place words and their icons (as originally)
教学楼 楼: a plain house. 图书馆: a book. 运动场: an oval. 体育馆: a dumbbell. 饭馆: a rice bowl. 超市: a cart. 银行 中国银行: a columned building. 商店 书店: a shop front. 邮局: an envelope with a red seal. 宿舍: a house with a bed. Several of these are ambiguous (a book for a library, the same shop front for a bookshop); the owner chose to keep them.
